About DFWhacks 2025

DFWhacks 2025 was an incredible celebration of creativity, collaboration, and the future of tech. On September 27th, 2025 at Collin College Frisco, 62 talented high school students came together to form teams, build innovative AI-powered applications, attend engaging workshops, and receive guidance from industry mentors. It was an unforgettable day filled with learning, innovation, and community building. Thank you to everyone who participated and made this event a tremendous success!

Event Highlights

A recap of our amazing day

Opening Ceremony

Started at 10:00 AM with an energizing welcome and challenge reveal

Participants learned about the AI challenge, formed teams, and kicked off their projects with enthusiasm and creativity.

Intense Coding

10 hours of non-stop innovation and collaboration

Teams worked tirelessly, building AI-powered applications with guidance from mentors and support from organizers.

Workshops & Mentorship

Learning opportunities throughout the day

Participants attended engaging workshops and received one-on-one mentorship from industry professionals.

Closing Ceremony

Celebrated achievements and awarded prizes at 7:00 PM

Teams showcased their amazing projects, winners were announced, and we celebrated everyone's hard work.
